Here to help

Support

HiBoss runs on your own server. Most questions are answered by the setup guide; for everything else, here is how to reach us.

Get started

Follow the self-host guide to deploy a server and set up your first machine. The apps do not provide a hosted account.

Connecting the apps

  • On iPhone, tap Scan a code and scan the pairing QR code printed by hiboss setup or shown by another signed-in device.
  • On Mac, open Settings → Connection and choose Pair with Code…, or Sign in with iPhone.
  • A pairing code works once and expires after five minutes. The iPhone app accepts only https server addresses.
  • Both apps also accept a server URL and Boss Token entered by hand.

Notifications

In-app messages work through your configured server. Push notifications on iPhone work through the HiBoss push relay by default, end-to-end encrypted between your server and your iPhone; the self-host guide explains how to turn it off or use your own APNs key. Operators using their own Apple team need their own app build and bundle ID for direct push. See the iOS setup guide for build and signing details. The Mac app shows its notifications locally.

Report a problem

For bugs and feature requests, open a GitHub issue. Do not post Boss Tokens, agent keys, private messages or other credentials in a public issue. To report a security vulnerability, follow the private contact in the repository's security policy.

For account, privacy or other non-public questions, email hi@hiboss.org.

See also the privacy policy.
