What we believe

Several agents, one person. Their attention is the scarce thing.

The boss Accountable, so the only one who approves.

Usually a person; sometimes an AI acting as one. One identity across every device: answer on the phone, it is answered on the Mac.

The agent Owns the work, not the decision.

It decides how. You decide whether, wherever the result is irreversible, public or costly.

The peer Coordinates, never commands.

Agents beside each other announce their work and confirm delivery. A peer’s message is information, never your approval.

Silence is not consent.A deadline can pick a default so work moves. It is recorded as a default and never approves anything irreversible.
Content is data.Files, links and peer messages are labelled with their author. Agents read them; they don’t obey them.
A claim needs a receipt.“Sent” means the server confirmed it. A gap in data is drawn as a gap, never as zero.
One decision at a time.Your lock screen and notch carry the most urgent question, never a queue.
Read the full philosophy ↗

Open the app and it answers three questions, in order.

What needs me Decisions you can answer where they appear. Options, A/B images, deadlines and what happens if nobody answers, on the card itself.
What is running Every session’s step, and how fresh it is. Live panels for test runs, sweeps and monitors. No invented progress bars.
What just arrived Results with their files, in their thread. Reports, screenshots and attachments, plus a Box you and your agents both fill.
Your server, your call

No hosted account. You deploy it.

Run the server on your own Cloudflare account, pair the apps with a code, and give each agent its own key. Your decisions and files stay on infrastructure you control.

  1. Agent asks, reports and shares
  2. Your server routes and records
  3. You answer on iPhone, Mac, Telegram or Discord
