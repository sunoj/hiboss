## English

**HiBoss 1.0: your agents ask, you decide.**

AI coding agents are good at the work and bad at knowing when to stop and ask. HiBoss gives them
one place to ask, and gives you one tap to answer.

- An agent such as Claude Code or Codex sends a question with options, context and an optional
  deadline. You answer on your iPhone, at the top of your Mac, or in Telegram or Discord. The first
  answer wins everywhere.
- Silence is never approval. A deadline can pick a default so work moves on, and HiBoss records it
  as a default, not as your answer.
- Live panels and a Progress timeline let you watch the work. Neither sends a notification.
- It runs on your own server: a Cloudflare Worker with D1 and R2 in your account. There is no
  HiBoss account, and no advertising or analytics in the apps.

HiBoss is open source under the MIT License. The server, the CLI and the Mac app are in the repository and its
releases; the iPhone and iPad app is coming to the App Store.

Start with the self-host guide: https://hiboss.org/docs/self-host/
Source: https://github.com/sunoj/hiboss

## 简体中文

**HiBoss 1.0：智能体来问，你来拍板。**

AI 编程智能体干活很在行，却不太知道什么时候该停下来问一句。HiBoss 给它们一个提问的地方，也让你点一下就能回答。

- Claude Code、Codex 这样的智能体提问时，会带上选项、背景和可选的截止时间。你在 iPhone 上、Mac 屏幕顶部，或者 Telegram、Discord 里回答，最先给出的答案在所有地方生效。
- 不回复，绝不算批准。到点可以自动选默认项，让工作继续；HiBoss 把它记为默认项，而不是你的回答。
- 实时面板和进展时间线让你随时看到进度，两者都不推送通知。
- 它运行在你自己的服务器上：你自己 Cloudflare 账号里的 Worker、D1 和 R2。没有 HiBoss 账号，应用里也没有广告和统计分析。

HiBoss 以 MIT 许可开源。服务器、命令行和 Mac 应用都在仓库及其发布页里，iPhone 和 iPad 应用即将登陆 App Store。

从自托管指南开始：https://hiboss.org/zh/docs/self-host/
源码：https://github.com/sunoj/hiboss
