Why the island

The boss is not absent. The boss is free.

Agents keep working; the laptop stays closed. HiBoss brings back only what needs the boss, so the rest of the time is theirs. The icon is the promise the product keeps.

These appear only where the brand speaks: the website, onboarding, the App Store and the all-clear state. In the working screens the four functional colours keep their single meanings, so a sky-blue panel never reads as “live”.

Using the icon

  • Use the artwork as is: no recolouring, cropping or redrawing.
  • Corner radius about 22% of its size; at 16 px it is still the icon, not a logo mark.
  • The icon itself stays an icon: app, header, menu bar, badges. Never decorate a decision with it.
  • Inside the product, speak its language instead: the island, palm, sea, deck chair and closed laptop, redrawn as scenes, by day or by night. “Nothing needs you” is the empty deck chair under the moon.
  • The same artwork on light and dark; it carries its own sky.
